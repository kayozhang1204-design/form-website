Case study Illustrative example

One launch.
Six formats.
A shared plan.

For fictional outdoor brand Field Notes, a seasonal launch becomes a way to connect creative decisions with customer acquisition.

The challenge

Each channel has its own creative, approvals, and reporting. The team needs to understand which messages bring in new customers.

The approach

Start with one brief. Adapt two creative ideas across formats, coordinate approvals, and agree on measurement before launch.

What to evaluate

Compare launch time and acquisition cost, then use a suitable controlled test to estimate incremental sales.

Scenario only. No customer results or performance improvements are claimed.
